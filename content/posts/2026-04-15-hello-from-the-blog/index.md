This is the first post on the example blog. It lives at `content/posts/2026-04-15-hello-from-the-blog.md` and is rendered to `/posts/hello-from-the-blog/` via the `post` layout.

The date in the filename becomes the publication date. You can also set it explicitly in front matter.

Tags from front matter automatically generate `/tags/<tag>/` index pages.
