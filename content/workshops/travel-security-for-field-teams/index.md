A practical, scenario-driven workshop for managers and team leads
sending people into complex environments. The session blends short
briefings with case studies drawn from real operations across Asia,
Africa, and the Middle East.

## What you'll learn

- Pre-deployment risk assessment frameworks
- Decision-making under information ambiguity
- Communication protocols when conventional comms fail
- When and how to escalate to evacuation

## Who it's for

Field-team leads, security focal points, programme managers, and HR
or operations staff responsible for staff safety in higher-risk
deployments. No security background required — the workshop assumes
generalist context.

## Format

Delivered via Zoom in a single 8-hour session (with breaks). Capped
at 18 participants to keep the discussion intimate. Pre-reading is
provided one week before the session; certificates of completion
issued afterwards.
