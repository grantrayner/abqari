A single intensive day on the communication side of a live incident:
who needs to hear what, in what order, and what gets left out for now.
The morning covers the principles — audiences, sequencing, the cost of
silence — and the afternoon is two extended role-plays where attendees
deliver actual briefings under timed pressure, with debriefs after each.

## What you'll learn

- A framework for sequencing internal, customer, and external briefings
- Drafting the first thirty minutes of a public statement
- Bridging from prepared lines to off-script questions
- Coordinating with legal and regulatory counsel under pressure
