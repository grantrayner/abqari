Two days on the craft of tabletop exercises: scoping, scenario design,
injects, room dynamics, and the debrief that turns a half-day exercise
into actionable changes. Day one is design; day two is facilitation,
with each attendee running a short scenario under observation.

## What you'll learn

- Scoping: matching exercise type to learning objective
- Scenario design: realism, branching points, and the inject calendar
- Room facilitation: pace, role-play, side conversations
- Debrief frameworks that produce changes, not just minutes
