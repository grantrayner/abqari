The deep-dive companion to **Crisis Response Foundations**, this
three-day workshop covers the design discipline behind a realistic
exercise: how scenarios are built, how injects are paced, how
controllers steer without spoiling, and how to extract durable
learning from the after-action review.

Best paired with the foundations workshop — attendees who complete
both leave with a working scenario library and the facilitation
craft to run their own exercises in-house. Available as an
in-person, fully-remote, or hybrid delivery.

## What you'll learn

- Scenario architecture: the master event list (MEL) and how it
  drives an exercise from start to finish
- Pacing injects so teams stay in the operating tempo without
  being overwhelmed
- Controller craft — the small interventions that keep simulations
  realistic without breaking immersion
- Designing the after-action review (AAR) for genuine learning,
  not blame
- Day 3 is hands-on: attendees design and deliver a 60-minute
  simulation, with peer critique

## Who it's for

Heads of security, crisis-response leads, and training managers
in multinational organisations — financial services, NGOs, and
manufacturing all well-represented in past cohorts.
