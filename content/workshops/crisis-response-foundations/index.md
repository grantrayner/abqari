Two days of structured exercises and discussion on building a crisis
response capability from the ground up. The workshop blends short
lectures with extended team exercises that put attendees inside the
operating tempo of a real crisis.

## What you'll learn

- Frameworks for building a crisis response capability
- Initial response and ongoing review processes
- Decision-making under time pressure
- Communicating with internal and external audiences

## Who it's for

Members of crisis response teams, security and ops leaders, and the
managers who back them up.
