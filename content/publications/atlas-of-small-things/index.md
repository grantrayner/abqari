A photographic atlas of the kind of places that get described in passing —
a market two streets back from the main road, a teashop where the same six
men have argued for thirty years, a stairwell whose tiles are slowly
losing their pattern. Eighty-eight photographs paired with short notes,
across four countries and seven years of wandering with a camera.

The notes don't try to explain the photographs. They sit alongside them
the way a caption to a museum object might — locating the moment without
overwriting it. Printed on uncoated stock so the images breathe instead
of glowing.
