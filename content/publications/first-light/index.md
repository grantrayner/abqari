A small book about beginnings. Twenty-two short pieces — call them
essays, observations, or notes; the form sits between all three —
written across two years of pre-dawn working sessions. The pieces are
loosely arranged by season, but they don't follow a plot; you can open
the book at any page and start there.

A companion volume, *Last Light*, follows the same form for the close
of the day. The two books are sold together as the *Light Notebooks*
series, but each reads as a complete object on its own.
