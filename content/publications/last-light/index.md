The second of the *Light Notebooks*. Same form as *First Light* —
short pieces, season-loose, readable in any order — but turned toward
endings rather than beginnings. The closing piece is a letter to the
author's own younger self, dated for the day the first book finished.

The two volumes are sold together as a set and read well in either
order. People who read *First Light* tend to read *Last Light* second;
people who came in through *Last Light* tend to flip backwards.
