Twelve essays written across five years, gathered for the first time. The
title comes from the slim margins of paperbacks where the author has, for
two decades, kept a running commentary with herself — questions, second
thoughts, references to look up later. *Margin Notes* is what those
margins might look like if the marginalia eventually outgrew the books.

Topics range from why interviewers ask the same three questions, to the
quiet failure mode of "more research," to what it means to actually finish
something. The essays are short — most under two thousand words — and
intended to be read out of order.
